RavenPack introduces NVIDIA Nemotron-powered pipeline for backtest-ready investment datasets

RavenPack has introduced a pipeline on Bigdata.com that turns its archive—more than 1 billion documents spanning 20+ years—into backtest-ready investment datasets, using an NVIDIA Nemotron model fine-tuned for financial labeling. RavenPack distilled NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super 120B into Nemotron 3 Nano 4B via LoRA and used it to label 845,220 excerpts from news and earnings transcripts covering 1,642 companies in 0.7 hours on two NVIDIA H200 GPUs: about 39× the teacher model’s throughput, at 63% lower full-history labeling cost and 96% lower cost on subsequent passes, while matching the teacher on 94.05% of chunk-level and 95.90% of sentence-level labels. The Bigdata.com search API, with bigdata-smart-batching, cut query counts by over 90%. In a worked example, a market-share gain/loss signal backtested from January 2016 to October 2026 produced an information ratio of 1.18 and 8.63% annualized excess return at sentence level, versus 0.41 and 3.78% for relevance-weighted sentiment alone. The pipeline is available today to Bigdata.com clients; Peter Hafez, RavenPack’s Chief Data Scientist, fronted the announcement. PR Newswire

Why it matters: RavenPack is industrializing dataset creation—converting a billion-document archive into backtest-ready signals at a fraction of prior labeling cost—which pressures MI’s own alternative-data and quantitative dataset economics and extends its token-priced content model into full signal production.

Executive readout

Eight signals that matter

“Own the data the agents cite” is becoming the market’s organizing principle.

Distribution is shifting from standalone terminals toward licensed data, tools and analytical skills embedded in Copilot, Gemini, ChatGPT and Amazon Quick.

01

Agent distribution is the land grab

LSEG, Moody’s, PitchBook and FactSet all expanded into third-party agent platforms in a single quarter. Absence from those surfaces risks losing the workflow entry point.

02

AlphaSense has momentum—and capital

A $350M raise, $600M+ ARR, an Accenture channel and multiple agentic launches make it the most acute private challenger.

03

ASKB resets desktop expectations

Bloomberg’s large beta, mobile continuity and portable BQL answers establish a new benchmark for research UX.

04

Partnerships may beat pure build

FactSet’s Google Cloud alliance will test whether a hyperscaler partnership can turn mid-single-digit ASV growth into acceleration.

05

AI-disruption pressure is financial

Elliott’s LSEG stake made the “AI commoditizes data” thesis an activist issue. LSEG’s reacceleration and buybacks offer a counter-playbook.

06

Private markets is the hot zone

Deal benchmarks, AI document ingestion and embedded diligence are converging around private-company workflows.

07

New agent economics are emerging

RavenPack’s token-priced content model targets the unit cost of AI research, not just the quality of retrieval.

08

Compliance is becoming a feature

YCharts ships disclosures and guardrails inside its agent. Advisor platforms will increasingly be judged on governed output, not novelty.
